- Worked directly with L3Harris senior engineers through Purdue’s Data Mine of the Rockies on satellite telemetry cybersecurity research
- Co-authored and presented “L3Harris: Satellite Cyberattack Detection” at the inaugural Data Mine of the Rockies Symposium in Colorado Springs to engineers and stakeholders from the U.S. Space Force, L3Harris, Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, CrowdStrike, and other aerospace and cybersecurity organizations
- Trained Isolation Forest and LSTM models on synthetic NOS3 satellite telemetry to detect and classify cyberattack anomalies
- Used Wireshark to identify affected devices and indicators of compromise in simulated satellite communications
- Mapped space-cyber scenarios to the SPARTA Matrix and MITRE ATT&CK frameworks, then generated synthetic telemetry for multiple attack types
