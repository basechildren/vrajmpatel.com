For CU Boulder’s Studio Lab, built a resumable Selenium crawler for the Parliament Digital Library, including dynamic pagination and saved session state. The research later continued remotely with the former Studio Lab PI after they moved to Princeton and concluded in May 2026. Processed 40+ GB of election speech audio from the 2014 and 2019 Modi and Gandhi campaigns through AWS Transcribe and Translate, and digitized Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha debates from 1981–2024. Parsed thousands of PDF statements with PyMuPDF and FuzzyWuzzy, then mapped OCR text to standardized ministry names.
