Built an interactive NLP system for intent classification and domain-specific responses. Fine-tuned LLaMA 3.2 1B with LoRA and QLoRA through Unsloth for clinical text generation, and trained BERT-style classifiers for mental-health labeling. The classifier reached 0.79 macro-F1 on aggregated clinical data. The training pipeline processed 53,000+ text samples.
