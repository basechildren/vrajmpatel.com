Academic NLP research project for intent classification and domain-specific response experiments. Fine-tuned LLaMA 3.2 1B with LoRA and QLoRA through Unsloth, and trained BERT-style classifiers for mental-health text labeling. The classifier reached 0.79 macro-F1 on aggregated research data. This was not a diagnostic, treatment, or clinical-decision product. The training pipeline processed 53,000+ text samples.
