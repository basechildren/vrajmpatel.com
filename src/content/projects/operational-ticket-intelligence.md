Built an internal ticket-classification platform for CU Boulder’s Institute of Behavioral Science. The system pairs FastAPI and PostgreSQL services with a React dashboard so staff can review operational trends alongside ML-assisted incident-tag suggestions.

The work focuses on reliable day-to-day use: keeping expensive model work off the interactive path, preserving review and approval boundaries around suggestions, and presenting staffing signals clearly enough to support operational decisions. A Recharts dashboard surfaced the Tuesday 10 a.m. ticket-volume peak used in staffing analysis.
